OpexAI changelog
================

1.0.0 (AI version 9) - unreleased
---------------------------------

First public release. Requires OpenTTD 15 or later (AI API 15).

- Multimodal company: railways, road vehicles (buses and trucks), aircraft
  and ships; each mode can be switched off.
- Routes compared across modes by expected return on investment, within the
  money available to the company.
- Construction of stations, track, roads, airports, docks and depots, then
  vehicle purchases and fleet growth on existing lines.
- Savegame support.
- Several OpexAI companies can share a game ("OpexAI", "OpexAI #2", ...).
- No dependency on AI libraries from Online Content.
- Seven settings: difficulty, use_trains, use_road_vehicles, use_aircraft,
  use_ships, show_signs, log_level. No debug signs and no decision log by
  default.
- Known limitation: tested only in the temperate climate, on maps from
  256x256 to 1024x1024 tiles, starting in 1970, with default OpenGFX
  vehicles and no NewGRFs.
